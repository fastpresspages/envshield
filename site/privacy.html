Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

1. Zero Telemetry & Local Execution

EnvShield CLI, MCP server, and GitHub Action operate completely locally on your hardware or CI runner. We do not collect, transmit, store, or analyze your environment variables, secrets, keys, or filenames.

2. In-Browser Simulator

The interactive simulator on the EnvShield website runs 100% client-side in your browser JavaScript runtime. Nothing entered into the simulator is ever transmitted over the network.

3. Contact Form

If you submit the contact form, your email and message are processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to forward the inquiry to meetcode99@gmail.com. We do not share or sell your email address.
